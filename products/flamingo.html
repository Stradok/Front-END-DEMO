Flamingo: an AI assistant that sits in your meetings

Flamingo is an AI meeting assistant that knows your project. It listens, keeps a live transcript and helps the meeting move.

Open Flamingo Book a meeting

What it does

  • Live transcript of the meeting
  • Answers with citations from your project material
  • Suggested questions to ask next
  • Notes and tasks written for you
  • Time estimates
  • Live UI prototypes drawn while the requirement is still being described
  • Give it a role, such as principal full-stack engineer, and it guides the meeting from that seat
  • Connects to the apps you already use

Who it is for

  • Founders and small teams without a senior engineer or designer in every meeting
  • Agencies and consultants who turn client conversations into specs
