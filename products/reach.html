Reach: your team's work, in one calm place

Reach is a team workspace we built for ourselves first and now offer to other teams. Shared boards and projects, tasks assigned to individuals, and a personal page for each person's own work.

Open Reach Book a meeting

What it does

  • Shared boards and projects for the whole team
  • Tasks assigned to a person or to a whole project
  • A personal "My work" page for each person
  • Progress notes on every task
  • GitHub commits logged onto projects
  • An activity log of what changed
  • Syncs live and works offline
  • Signs in with an emailed code, so there are no passwords to manage

Who it is for

  • Small teams that want one place for projects, tasks and progress
  • Software teams that want commits to show up next to the work
  • Anyone tired of passwords and of tools that stop working offline
