The problem

When leads live in phones, inboxes and spreadsheets, nobody knows what is waiting for a reply. A shared pipeline turns that into a clear list.

What we build

  • A pipeline that matches your real stages, from enquiry to completed job
  • Leads captured automatically from calls, messages and your website
  • Follow-up tasks and reminders so nothing is forgotten
  • AI help for summaries, replies and next-step suggestions
  • Industry CRMs: we have built CRMs for specific trades

Who it's for

  • Owners who can't see where leads and quotes stand
  • Teams using spreadsheets and chat apps as a CRM
  • Businesses in trades that want a CRM shaped around their jobs
