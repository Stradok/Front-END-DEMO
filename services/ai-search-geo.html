The problem

AI assistants and search engines build their answers from what they can read and verify. If your site is vague, thin or inconsistent with your other listings, you are easy to skip.

What we build

  • Clear, structured pages that answer the questions your customers ask
  • Structured data so machines understand who you are, what you offer and where
  • Consistent business details across your site and listings
  • Content written to be quoted: direct answers, specifics, real names
  • A review of how you currently appear in search and AI assistants

Who it's for

  • Businesses that want to be recommended, not just listed
  • Companies whose competitors already show up in AI answers
  • Anyone with a good service and a weak online description
