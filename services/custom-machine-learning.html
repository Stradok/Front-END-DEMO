Machine learning built on your own data

Models and computer-vision systems trained on your records, photos and documents, so the result is specific to your business.

The problem

Many businesses have a person making the same judgment call over and over: checking, sorting, forecasting, approving. If you hold records of past cases, a trained model can do it or support it.

What we build

  • Computer vision: object detection and counting, visual quality inspection, OCR and document extraction, video and camera analytics
  • Predictive models: demand and sales forecasting, churn and lead scoring, anomaly and fraud detection, recommendation systems
  • Language and speech: custom AI assistants on your data, text classification and sentiment, speech-to-text and call analysis
  • Deployment and monitoring so models keep working after launch

Who it's for

  • Businesses with a repeated decision and records of past cases
  • Operations that inspect, count or read lots of images or documents
  • Teams that want forecasts or scores from their own history

How it starts

Every project starts with one scoping conversation and ends in a fixed price, not a generic rate card. We don't quote numbers before we understand your business.

Questions

How do I know if ML is the right tool?
